# Code of Conduct

Plumbline is maintained by people who give their time to it. We expect
everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussions)
to make that a good experience for others.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate, including when you disagree.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming.
- Welcome newcomers. Many COBOL developers are new to open source, and
  many open-source developers are new to COBOL.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory remarks of any kind.
- Personal attacks or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing others' private information without their permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or other contributions that
violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban people who do so
repeatedly or seriously.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through GitHub (see
the maintainer list in the repository README). Reports are handled
confidentially.
